Everything under [Account](/account) is scoped to the account you are signed
in as. There is no way to look up somebody else's.

## Changing your password

Under [Security](/account/security). You are asked for the current password
as well as the new one, which is what stops somebody who finds your machine
unlocked from locking you out of your own account.

Changing the password does not sign you out of the game. If you are changing
it because you think somebody else has it, close the game as well.

## Changing your e-mail address

Also under Security. Depending on how this server is set up, the new address
may have to be confirmed by a code sent to it before the change takes effect —
which means a typo cannot strand your account on an address that does not
exist.

The e-mail address is how a password reset reaches you, so it is worth keeping
current even if you never otherwise use it.

## Your history

[Account history](/account/history) shows what has happened to your account:

- **Panel sign-ins** — when this website was signed into, and from where.
- **Game sign-ins** — the same for the game itself.
- **Password changes**, **password resets** and **e-mail changes** — when
  each happened.

This is the page to look at if something feels wrong. A sign-in you do not
recognise, from a place you have never been, is worth acting on: change the
password, and tell the server's staff.

What it shows is addresses and times, not passwords. Nobody — including the
staff — can read your password out of this panel.
