## The ladders

[Rankings](/rankings/level) covers the ladders this panel can build from the
server's own data — character level, wealth, crafting, and whichever others
this server has enabled. Guild ladders are listed beside the character ones.

They are read from live data rather than from a snapshot, so a ladder is
current as of the moment you loaded it, within the same caveat as a character
page: a figure that only gets written when a character logs off will lag until
it does.

## Staying off the wealth ladder

Some servers let a player hide from the Zeny ranking. Where that is available
it is a preference on your own account rather than something you have to ask
staff for, and it removes you from the listing rather than rounding your
number off.

## Server statistics

The front page carries the counts that describe the world as a whole: how many
accounts and characters exist, how many people are online, and which maps they
are on. [Who is online](/who-is-online) is the same thing at the level of
individual characters, for everyone who has not hidden themselves.
