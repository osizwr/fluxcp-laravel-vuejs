## Where to ask

Wherever this server takes questions — a Discord server, a forum, a support
form on this site. The links in the footer and the navigation are the current
ones; a link somebody sent you two years ago may not be.

## What to include

Most of the back-and-forth in a support thread is the people answering trying
to work out what actually happened. You can skip all of it:

- **Your account name.** Not your password. Never your password.
- **The character involved**, if it is about a character.
- **What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead** — in that
  order, as three plain sentences.
- **When it happened**, roughly, with your timezone. Server logs are searched
  by time.
- **A screenshot**, if there was anything on the screen. The whole window,
  including any error text, rather than a crop of the part you think matters.

## What support cannot do

- **Recover an account without its e-mail address.** There is no way to
  establish that an account is yours without it, and an exception made for
  you is an exception available to whoever asks next.
- **Tell you your password.** It is not readable by anybody.
- **Reverse something that happened in the game** — a trade, a death, a
  mistaken sale — unless this server has a stated policy that says otherwise.

## Reporting a security problem

If you find a way to see or do something you should not be able to, report it
privately to the server's staff rather than posting it. That is true of bugs
in the game and of problems with this website.
